The Value of Trees

Trees are essential for life on Earth. They absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen, helping to keep the air clean. Their roots hold the soil together and reduce erosion during heavy rain. Trees also provide shade, fruit and shelter for birds and other animals. In cities, parks and roadside trees can make neighbourhoods cooler and more pleasant. However, forests are often cut down to make space for buildings and roads. This can destroy wildlife habitats and increase environmental problems. Everyone can help by planting trees, caring for young plants and avoiding unnecessary waste of paper. Schools and communities can organise tree-planting days to encourage people to protect nature. Even small actions, when repeated by many people, can make a positive difference.

1.How do trees help keep the air clean?

2.How do tree roots help the soil?

3.Name two benefits trees provide to animals or people.

4.What can happen when forests are cut down?

5.Write two actions students can take to protect trees.
